# Project context

prediction-market-agent focuses on sports arbitrage research across prediction
venues. It is independent of airport-data-agent. The current mode is monitoring,
evidence review, and paper signals; automated trading is disabled.

The user chose the connected Predictefy/Kalshi example as a visual and operational
template. That example remains isolated under apps/kalshi-btc15m. Its BTC Gaussian
model, five-minute snapshot, and stake policy are not sports business rules.
The unrelated wallet copy-bot has been retired into a source-only archive.

The proposed system has ResearchGraph, ObservationGraph, SignalGraph, and a future
LearningGraph. Transport, data cleaning, payoff coverage, fees, depth, and risk are
deterministic. AI specialists propose rules matches and explain stored evidence;
human review is required for unresolved equivalence. All active text uses English.

Bronze raw observations, Silver canonical records/snapshots, and Gold signals have
a proposed SQLite schema. LangGraph runtime, checkpoints, database deployment,
sports book adapters, and the signal engine are not implemented. The offline CLI
validates the registry and graph blueprint without external actions.

A manual Predictefy catalog authentication was verified on October 3, 2026. This
is not sports capability, contract equivalence, book, or signal verification.
The first sport/competition, fixture, and additional venues remain to be selected.
Polymarket and Kalshi are proposed starting venues. Reference/soft speed labels
remain hypotheses to measure, not market facts.

remain local and are never part of this context, datasets, prompts, or archives.
