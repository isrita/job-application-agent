# Introduction to Career Skills in Data Analytics

Issued by LinkedIn Learning. Validates essential competencies in translating business queries into analytical workflows, evaluating analytical methods, and structuring quantitative insights for stakeholder communication.

## Key Competencies
- **Analytical Problem Solving:** Framing business problems into structured analytical hypotheses.
- **Data Lifecycle Understanding:** Data gathering, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and reporting.
- **Stakeholder Communication:** Presenting metric trends, dashboards, and quantitative insights to non-technical business partners.
